About

Research at the intersection of behavior, politics, and data.

I study how people and political actors respond to their social and institutional environments. My work combines experimental and quasi-experimental research designs with text, audio, video, and survey data.

I am also a Research Associate at the Chair of Data Science in Business and Economics at the University of Würzburg and a Junior Researcher at C-BEAM.

Job Market Paper Manuscript in preparation

Facing Right-Wing Populists: Video Evidence on Elite Behavioral Responses in Parliament

Democratic institutions rely not only on formal rules, but also on restraint and deliberation among political elites. We study how established politicians respond when a right-wing populist party enters a national legislature. Combining synchronized text, audio, and video from several thousand plenary speeches with variation in exposure generated by parliamentary committee assignments, we examine verbal engagement, visual attention, bodily gestures, and vocal delivery.

Working Paper

Digital Skills: Social Disparities and the Impact of Early Mentoring

with Fabian Kosse and Arna Woemmel

We study social disparities in digital skills, focusing on proficiency and confidence, and use a long-run randomized intervention to examine the effects of an enriched childhood social environment.

Accommodation of Right-Wing Populist Rhetoric: Evidence from Parliamentary Speeches in Germany

with Emilio Esguerra, Felix Hagemeister, and Julian Heid

We provide evidence on how exposure to right-wing populist rhetoric affects the language used by mainstream politicians in the German parliament.

Work in Progress

Who Looks Up to Whom? Role Model Selection Amongst Teenagers: News Coverage Paired with Natural Language Processing

with Fabian Kosse

We link longitudinal survey data on teenagers and their families to a multilingual corpus of news coverage to study how adolescents choose public-figure role models and how their social environments shape that choice.
